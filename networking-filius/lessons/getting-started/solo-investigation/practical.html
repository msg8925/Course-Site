PART B

ping and ARP

Pair up with a partner. Exchange IP addresses, then run ping <partner's IP address>.

1. How many replies did you receive, and were any packets lost?
2. What does the TTL (Time To Live) value tell you?
3. Run arp -a before and after the ping. What new entry appeared, and why?
PART C

GUI vs CMD

Find your IP settings via Windows Settings > Network & Internet. Compare what you see here to your ipconfig /all output.

Which is easier to read quickly, and which gives more detail? Give one reason for your answer.
PART D

Independent task: annotate your findings

On a blank sheet (or below), sketch or paste a screenshot of your ipconfig /all output. Label each field in your own words — don't just copy the textbook definition, explain what it tells you about your device.
