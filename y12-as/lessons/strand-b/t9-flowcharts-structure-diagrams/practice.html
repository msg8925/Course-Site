Structure diagrams

Test your understanding of what a structure diagram does (and doesn't) show.

8

True or false: a structure diagram shows the order in which tasks are carried out.

Show full working

False. A structure diagram only shows how a program is broken into modules — it says nothing about the order or logic. That's what flowcharts are for.

E

Exam-style question

A longer, multi-part question.

13

A car park system needs to: (1) read a car's entry time when it arrives, (2) read the exit time when it leaves, (3) calculate the parking duration, (4) calculate a fee based on that duration, (5) display the fee.

(a) For each of steps 1–5, state which flowchart symbol you would use.
(b) Suggest a structure diagram breakdown of this system into modules, including at least one module with its own sub-module.

Show model answer

(a) Step 1 (read entry time): input/output parallelogram. Step 2 (read exit time): input/output parallelogram. Step 3 (calculate duration): process rectangle. Step 4 (calculate fee): process rectangle. Step 5 (display fee): input/output parallelogram.

(b) A reasonable breakdown: top module "Car Park System," with sub-modules "Record Entry," "Record Exit," and "Calculate Fee." "Calculate Fee" could itself have a sub-module such as "Apply Discount" (e.g. for members), showing a second level of decomposition. Any similarly reasonable module breakdown is acceptable — there's no single correct answer, as long as it's a hierarchy of modules with no logic or ordering shown.
