E

Exam-style questions

A university is deciding whether to store its student records on a public cloud or a private cloud. Discuss one factor in favour of each option, and recommend which one the university should choose.

Reveal model answer

In favour of public cloud: lower cost, since the university pays only for the storage it uses without needing to buy and maintain its own dedicated infrastructure, and capacity can scale easily as student numbers change.

In favour of private cloud: student records are sensitive personal data, often subject to data protection regulations — private cloud gives the university full control over exactly where that data is stored and who can access it.

Recommendation: private cloud, since the sensitivity and legal protection requirements around student records generally outweigh the cost savings of public cloud for this kind of data.

A company is connecting two buildings 500 metres apart, across land it does not own, so no cable can be laid between them. Explain which transmission medium is most suitable, and why fibre-optic cable — despite its excellent speed — would not be a realistic choice here.

Reveal model answer

Most suitable: microwave. A point-to-point microwave link needs no physical cable at all — only a clear line of sight between the two buildings — so it avoids the problem of not being able to lay cable across land the company doesn't own entirely.

Why not fibre-optic: fibre-optic is a wired medium — it still requires a physical cable to be laid along the entire route between the two buildings. Since the company doesn't own the land in between and cannot lay cable across it, fibre-optic's speed advantage is irrelevant here; the medium simply cannot be installed at all.
