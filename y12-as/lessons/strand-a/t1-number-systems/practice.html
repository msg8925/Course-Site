Denary ↔ binary

A quick recap before we extend to hex.

1

Convert 91 to 8-bit binary.

Show full working
91 ÷ 2 = 45 remainder 1 45 ÷ 2 = 22 remainder 1 22 ÷ 2 = 11 remainder 0 11 ÷ 2 = 5 remainder 1 5 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 1 2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0 1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

Reading bottom to top: 01011011. Check: 64 + 16 + 8 + 2 + 1 = 91.

2

Convert 11010110 to denary.

Show full working
128 + 64 + 0 + 16 + 0 + 4 + 2 + 0 = 214

Answer: 214.

B

Denary ↔ hex

Use repeated division by 16, or the place-value sum, as shown in the explainer.

3

Convert 173 to hexadecimal.

Show full working
173 ÷ 16 = 10 remainder 13 (D) 10 ÷ 16 = 0 remainder 10 (A)

Reading bottom to top: AD. Check: (10 × 16) + 13 = 173.

4

Convert hex 4E to denary.

Show full working
(4 × 16) + (14 × 1) = 64 + 14 = 78

Answer: 78.
